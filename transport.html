Taxis & Your Return Journey

As Birtsmorton Court is situated in a rural location, we strongly recommend that you consider your return journey before the end of the evening.

The following taxi companies are familiar with Birtsmorton Court. Please order your taxi to arrive by the venue closing time of 12:00am.

Please be aware that if taxis haven't arrived after the venue has closed, you will be asked to wait in the car park.
